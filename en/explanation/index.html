Explanation

Texts to understand Synesis: the why, the context and the ideas behind the tools

These texts explain why Synesis is the way it is. They are not step-by-step material (for that, see the Tutorials) nor recipes for a task (see the How-to Guides). Choose by the question you have.

I want to understand what Synesis is

  • Synesis 101 course (videos): short videos explaining Synesis from scratch (in Portuguese).
  • What Synesis can express: a panoramic view of the annotation possibilities.
  • Why structured annotation matters: rigor, freedom and the future of qualitative knowledge.
  • Why not just do it in Excel?: an honest answer about when Synesis makes sense and when it does not.
  • How Synesis came about: the research problem that gave rise to the language.
  • The architectural position: how Synesis balances readability for people and rigor for machines.

I want to code with method

  • Coding methods in Synesis: expressing qualitative coding traditions with templates.
  • AI-assisted coding: a real case of bibliometric analysis.
    • Research metrics
    • Comparison of AI models

I want to connect knowledge and AI

  • Knowledge graphs and GraphRAG: why knowledge graphs transform qualitative research.
  • Vector embeddings and GraphRAG: what semantic search adds to retrieval, measured rather than estimated.
    • How the benchmark was run

I want to see Synesis in my field

  • Law: legal knowledge as auditable institutional heritage.
  • Scripture: systematic annotation of sacred texts.
  • Participatory socio-environmental diagnosis: coding and SWOT analysis in community diagnoses.

I want to know how the tool works inside

  • Inside the compiler: how text becomes structured knowledge, stage by stage.
  • The LSP interface: how the compiler comes alive in the editor.
  • Roadmap and ideas: proposals for extending the language that are not implemented yet.
